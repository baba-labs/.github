# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in BaBa Labs projects — as a colleague, contributor, customer or
passer-by filing an issue — to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and considerate about the person. Strong disagreement about a
  technical decision is welcome; contempt for the person holding it is not.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. A review comment is about the code.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, apologise, and move on.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until you have real
  reason to think otherwise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion
- Anything a reasonable professional would regard as inappropriate at work

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions —
and whenever someone is representing BaBa Labs in public.

## Reporting

Report to <conduct@baba-labs.com>. Reports are handled by the company's director and
treated confidentially. We will acknowledge within 3 business days.

We are a small company with a single director, who also reads that mailbox. If your
report concerns the director, you can raise it with GitHub instead using
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam),
which are handled independently of BaBa Labs.

## Enforcement

We will respond proportionately: a private word, a formal warning, a temporary
restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces. We will explain the decision to the
person reporting, within the limits of others' privacy.
