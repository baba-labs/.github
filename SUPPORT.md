# Support

Where to go depends on who you are.

## Clients with a support agreement

Use the channel agreed in your contract — it is the only route with a response commitment
attached, and it reaches someone with context on your deployment.

If you have lost the details, email <hello@baba-labs.com> from an address we know.

## Prospective customers and commercial enquiries

<hello@baba-labs.com>.

## Security vulnerabilities

Do not use the channels above, and do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Bugs and questions on public repositories

Open an issue on the repository concerned. Please check existing issues first.

We maintain public repositories on a best-effort basis. There is no response
commitment, and there will be periods where a release takes priority over an issue
queue. If something is blocking you and you need it resolved to a timescale, that is a
commercial conversation rather than an issue — <hello@baba-labs.com>.

## What we cannot help with

- Debugging your own Azure configuration where the problem is not in our product
- Anything requiring access to your production data. Where our software is built so that
  we *cannot* see it, that is deliberate, and diagnostics are designed to work without it —
  send the diagnostic output rather than production data.

If you are ever asked for production data by someone claiming to be BaBa Labs, treat it as
suspicious and contact us through a channel you already know.
