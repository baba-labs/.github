## BaBa Labs

**Brewing Innovation. Scaling Solutions.**

We design and build cloud-native systems — architecture, implementation, and the
delivery pipeline that keeps them shipping.

### What we do

**Architecture** — system and solution design for teams who have outgrown the thing that
got them here. Service boundaries, data models, messaging, and the trade-offs written
down so the next person understands why.

**Cloud-native implementation** — .NET and Azure. Backend services, APIs, event-driven
and messaging architectures, built to run in production rather than to demo.

**CI/CD and Infrastructure as Code** — pipelines, quality gates, and infrastructure that
is reviewed and versioned like everything else. If a deployment is a ritual performed by
one person, that is the problem we fix first.

**Product engineering** — end to end, from an idea to something with users on it.

### How we work

Our engineering conventions are public, because they are not a secret and because
clients ask:

- [Working conventions](https://github.com/baba-labs/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) — branching, commits, review, quality gates
- [Security policy](https://github.com/baba-labs/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) — vulnerability disclosure, with response targets we can actually meet
- [Support](https://github.com/baba-labs/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md)

Most of our repositories are private. The public ones are shared engineering
infrastructure — pipeline templates and project scaffolds — maintained on a best-effort
basis.
